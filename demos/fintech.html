Security

What we run on our side, and who picks up when something looks wrong.

  • 2-factor on every login

    Authenticator app, passkey or hardware key. It cannot be turned off, for any role.

    Audited every year by an independent firm. Ask in the app and the current report arrives under NDA within one business day.

  • FDIC insurance through partner banks

    Deposits are held at partner banks, Members FDIC, and are eligible for FDIC insurance up to the stated limit.

    (619) 555-0142, answered 24 hours a day by our own staff. Any admin can freeze a card or a user from the app while on the call.

Is Ledgerly a bank?

No. Ledgerly is a financial technology company. Your deposits are held at partner banks, Members FDIC, and the account and routing numbers you receive belong to those banks.

What does an account cost?

There is no monthly fee and no minimum balance. ACH and domestic wires are $0, and an outgoing international wire is $15. The full table is under Fees.

How is the 3.9% APY paid?

Interest accrues daily on the full savings balance and is paid on the first business day of each month. The rate is 3.9% APY as of October 2026 and rates may change.

Which accounting software does it sync with?

QuickBooks, Xero and NetSuite. Transactions post overnight with the category, the receipt and the name of the approver attached.

Who can approve a payment?

Anyone you give the approver role. Rules can depend on the amount, the payment type and the account, and a requester can never approve their own payment.
