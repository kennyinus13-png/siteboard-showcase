The place

Dune House is a row of private villas on the seaward side of Scenic Highway 30A in Santa Rosa Beach, on the dune ridge above the Gulf.

Each villa is a separate house with its own gate, kitchen, terrace and pool. A boardwalk runs from the gardens over the dune to the beach, two minutes on foot, where loungers and towels are set out from 08:00 until sunset.

We built the first villa in 2014 and still run all of them ourselves. You book with us on this page or by phone. There is no platform in between, so there is no service fee, and you can move your dates free of charge up to 14 days before arrival.

Marguerite and Tom Ellery, owners

After dark

The pool lights stay on until midnight. After that only the path lights are lit, low and amber, because sea turtles nest on this beach from May to October.

A cook comes to the villa at 19:00 if you ask by noon the day before. Dinner is $95 a person and the menu depends on what the boats brought in that morning.

There is no reception desk. Text (850) 555-0193 and one of us answers, usually within ten minutes, until 23:00.
