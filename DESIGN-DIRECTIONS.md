# Industry design directions

The bar for every template: **a buyer should only need to swap the photos and the words.**
That means the bones — colour, type, sections, imagery style — must already speak the industry's language.
This file sets that language per industry group, for upgrading existing templates and building new ones.

How to read each entry:
- **People come to check** — the one or two questions a visitor wants answered in 5 seconds. The template must answer them above the fold.
- **Trust comes from** — what makes this business feel legitimate in this industry.
- **Photos** — the kind of photography the fixed layout should be designed around (so a buyer's own photos drop in naturally).
- **Mood** — colour/type direction for the non-editable bones.

---

## Food & hospitality (bakery, café, restaurant, food truck, roaster — "Cafés", "Restaurants")
- People come to check: **what's on the menu today, hours, where**.
- Trust comes from: today-ness (a dated board, "baked this morning"), real prices, a human voice.
- Photos: close-up product shots in natural light, texture-heavy (crumb, steam, crust). Layout should frame 1 hero food close-up + small square product shots.
- Mood: warm neutrals (cream, flour-white, charcoal), one appetite accent (ember orange / crust brown). Serif or humanist type for warmth. Never corporate blue.

## Beauty & salons (hair, skin, pilates/yoga adjacent — "Beauty", "Salons")
- People come to check: **price list and how to book**.
- Trust comes from: a clean service+price table, before/after or finished-look photos, stylist names.
- Photos: editorial portraits, result shots on plain backgrounds; the layout should treat photos like a lookbook (tall crops, generous whitespace).
- Mood: high-contrast black/white with one muted accent (blush, sage). Fashion-adjacent type (tight tracking, light weights).

## Clinics & health (dental, hospital, vet, counseling, pharmacy — "Clinics", "Wellness", "Pharmacy")
- People come to check: **can I get an appointment, do they take my insurance, where**.
- Trust comes from: hours incl. evenings/weekends, insurance list, doctor names + credentials, a calm tone. No stock-smile overload.
- Photos: bright interiors, staff portraits (approachable, not posed), equipment only as background.
- Mood: airy white + one calm colour (teal, soft green). Rounded corners, generous spacing. Type: clear, slightly rounded sans. Accessibility (contrast, size) matters double here.

## Fitness (gym, pilates, golf, wearables — "Fitness")
- People come to check: **schedule, membership price, vibe of the space**.
- Trust comes from: a real timetable, coach names, transparent tiers.
- Photos: dark, high-contrast action shots OR serene studio shots — pick per sub-industry (gym=bold dark, pilates/yoga=light calm) and keep the layout committed to it.
- Mood: gyms: near-black with a hot accent (volt, red). Studios: warm light neutrals. Big condensed type for gyms, soft type for studios.

## Fashion & retail (clothing, sneakers, jewelry, menswear, bags — "Fashion", "Resale")
- People come to check: **the products, right now — new arrivals, the look**.
- Trust comes from: strong product grid, consistent photography, clear prices, drops/newness signals.
- Photos: products on consistent backgrounds; one campaign/lookbook hero. The grid IS the design.
- Mood: minimal mono (black/white/grey), type does the branding (logotype-ish headers). Jewelry/luxury: more whitespace, serif, gold/ivory restraint.

## Home & objects (furniture, bedding, florist, design objects — "Home")
- People come to check: **does their taste match mine; materials and dimensions**.
- Trust comes from: material/dimension specs, maker story, room-context photos.
- Photos: in-situ room scenes + clean object shots; layout alternates the two.
- Mood: warm paper tones, muted earth palette, quiet serif headings. Lots of air.

## Professional services (law, tax, consulting, real estate, marketing — "Law", "Consulting", "Real estate", "Marketing")
- People come to check: **do they handle my problem, who are these people, how do fees work**.
- Trust comes from: named people with faces, practice areas as plain language, fee transparency, results/cases.
- Photos: real team portraits > skyline stock. Layout should make the people section unavoidable.
- Mood: restrained navy/ink + paper white; one confident accent. Strong serif or grotesque headings. Feels printed, not "techy".

## Trades & industrial (solar, CNC, recycling, cleaning, water — "Industrial")
- People come to check: **do they do my job, service area, how do I get a quote**.
- Trust comes from: a quote form that asks the right questions, capability tables with numbers, certifications, before/after jobs.
- Photos: real work in progress (hands, machines, sites) — grit is good. Layout leads with the quote path.
- Mood: utilitarian — steel grey, safety accent (orange/yellow) used sparingly. Tabular numbers, mono details.

## Tech & SaaS (dev tools, AI, fintech, security, productivity — "Digital", "AI", "Tools", "Productivity", "Fintech", "Security")
- People come to check: **what does it do (in one sentence), what does it look like, pricing**.
- Trust comes from: a real product screenshot front and centre, logos/integrations, honest pricing table, docs link.
- Photos: none — product UI is the imagery. The fixed layout must make the product window the hero.
- Mood: per positioning — dev tools: dark + mono accents; fintech: trust-light with ink; AI: restrained (avoid gradient-slop). Type: tight sans + mono details.

## Creative studios & agencies (branding, interactive, motion, architecture, portfolio — "Branding", "Interactive", "Motion", "Architecture", "Portfolio", "Product")
- People come to check: **the work. Nothing else matters until the work convinces**.
- Trust comes from: 4–8 strong cases with outcomes, client names, awards.
- Photos: full-bleed project imagery; layout gets out of the way (index-like navigation is a feature).
- Mood: extreme restraint or one loud signature move — never middle. Typography-led.

## Editorial (magazines, blogs, news, newsletter, archives, video — "Magazines", "News", "Newsletter", "Archives", "Video", "Books")
- People come to check: **is there something good to read right now**.
- Trust comes from: fresh dated content, clear sections, a voice.
- Photos: editorial art direction; layout = real typographic hierarchy (kickers, decks, bylines).
- Mood: print DNA — serif headlines, rules/borders, restrained palette with section colours.

## Education & community (language school, daycare, nonprofit — "Education", "Nonprofit")
- People come to check: **schedule/program, cost, how to join or give**.
- Trust comes from: real faces, outcomes ("students placed", "wells built"), safety/credential notes for kids.
- Photos: candid people-in-action; warmth over polish.
- Mood: friendly colour (but max 2), round shapes OK for kids, clean type elsewhere. Nonprofit: impact numbers big.

## Stays & venues (hotel, villa, wedding — "Hotels", "Venues", "Weddings")
- People come to check: **what it looks like, dates/price, how to book**.
- Trust comes from: honest big photography, a booking bar that works, rate table, map.
- Photos: light-filled wide interiors + detail vignettes; layout is photography-first.
- Mood: quiet luxury — ivory, stone, one deep accent. Serif headings, wide spacing.
